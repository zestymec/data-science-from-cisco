Types of Data

Data is all around us. It can be quantitative or qualitative, and discrete or continuous.

Discrete data

Collected by counting. Whole, concrete numbers, often prefixed with “the number of”.

  • Customers who bought bicycles
  • Employees in each department
  • Diesel fuel purchased each week for a delivery truck

Continuous data

Collected by measuring. Includes complex, fluctuating numbers.

  • Temperature inside the store
  • Speed of a rider in a race
  • Distance travelled by a moving bicycle
