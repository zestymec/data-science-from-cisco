03 · Variety of data

Tidy tables vs messy real life

Structured data fits neatly in rows and columns and lives in relational databases. Unstructured data (photos, video, emails, posts) has no fixed shape and is often kept in non-relational (NoSQL) databases.

🗂️ Structured · relational database

IDNameCityAge 1AliLahore212SaraKarachi243OmarMultan30

Fixed schema. Easy to search with SQL (MySQL, PostgreSQL).

🌪️ Unstructured · NoSQL / data lake

📷🎵✉️🎬💬📄

No fixed format. Rich but harder to analyse (MongoDB, Cassandra).

Also: JSON and XML are semi-structured: they have keys or tags but no rigid table.

05 · Big data

The four Vs

Big data’s characteristics change how data is collected, transmitted, stored and accessed. Each V creates a challenge for data infrastructure engineers.

📦 Volume

How much data. Needs scalable, distributed storage.

⚡ Velocity

How fast it arrives and must be processed.

🎨 Variety

Many formats and sources.

📝🖼️🎥📡🧾

🔍 Veracity

🛒Retail

🚦Transport

Better yields, early warnings.

Takeaway: veracity is the V analysts forget. Bad data in means bad decisions out.
06 · Managing big data

The data pipeline

A pipeline moves data reliably from where it is created to where it is used, in three phases: ingestion, transformation and storage.

📥

1 · Ingestion

Collect from apps, sensors, databases and APIs, in batches or streams.

🧹

2 · Transformation

Clean, filter, validate, reformat and combine.

🏛️

3 · Storage

Keep in a database, warehouse or data lake for analysts and models.

Takeaway: data scientists often spend most of their time in the transformation (cleaning) phase.

What is machine learning?

                        
AI is the broad goal of machines doing tasks that need human
                            intelligence. Machine learning is a subset where algorithms learn patterns from data
                            instead of following hand-written rules. A classifier is an algorithm that sorts new
                            items into categories.

                    

                


                
Types of machine learning analysis

                
Tap a card to see how each one works.

                

                    

                        
🏷️ Supervised

                        
🐱+ label “cat” →🧠→🐶?

                        
Learns from labelled examples (inputs with the right answer) then predicts
                            labels for new data. Used for classification (spam or not) and regression (house price).

                    

                    

                        
🔎 Unsupervised

                        

                        
Gets no labels and finds hidden structure on its own, such as grouping
                            customers with similar behaviour (clustering).

                    

                    

                        
🎮 Reinforcement

                        
🤖action →🌍← reward

                        
An agent learns by trial and error, getting rewards for good actions and
                            penalties for bad ones. Used in game AI and robotics.

AI: fact and fiction

                

                    

                        
FACT
AI is already used in search, translation, fraud detection and
                        recommendations.
                    

                    

                        
FACT
AI quality depends on the quality and quantity of its training data.
                    

                    

                        
FICTION
AI “thinks” like a human. Today’s systems find statistical
                        patterns.
                    

                    

                        
FICTION
AI is always objective. It can inherit bias from its data.
